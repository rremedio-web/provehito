# Code of Conduct

Participation in this project requires respectful, constructive, and
professional conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, documentation, and ideas without attacking people.
- Be clear about evidence, uncertainty, and technical trade-offs.
- Welcome different backgrounds and communication styles.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and the project’s local-security limits.
- Accept good-faith correction and keep reviews focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption,
unwanted contact, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing another
person’s private information are not acceptable. Attempts to bypass project
authority or security controls in order to affect another person or system are
also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the project’s
current private communication channel. Do not publish sensitive details in a
public issue. Maintainers may ask for context, protect the privacy of people
involved, and take proportionate action, including a warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Retaliation against a good-faith report is prohibited. Reports are handled
with discretion, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where action or law
requires disclosure.
